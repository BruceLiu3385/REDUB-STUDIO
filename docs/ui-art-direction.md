# Cyberpunk studio artwork

The UI uses an original AI-generated artwork as a decorative layer. Controls, text, status, and video playback remain real HTML elements.

- Generation mode: built-in `image_gen`, new image, opaque background. No external API/CLI or reference images.
- Asset: `apps/redub_studio/redub_studio/static/assets/neural-voice.webp`
- Dimensions: 1536 × 1024 pixels.
- Delivery: the generated PNG was converted to WebP at quality 91 without cropping, resizing, or content edits.
- Visual direction: near-black, cyan, electric violet, tiny acid-green signals; a holographic voice profile positioned to the right with dark negative space to the left.

Subject: a luminous translucent profile facing left, placed in the rightmost half, interconnected flowing cyan and electric-violet wave ribbons that feel like voice synthesis traveling through a neural mesh; tiny acid-green accents.
Style/medium: polished cinematic 3D scientific visualization with glassy iridescence, ultra-fine mesh details, crisp luminous filaments, realistic bloom and careful depth of field.
Composition/framing: horizontal landscape, about 1536 by 1024; main subject confined to the right 55 percent; left 40 percent is clean very dark negative space for real HTML copy; generous dark edges so it can crop gracefully.
Lighting/mood: intense cyan and electric violet rim lights against near-black, a little mint signal light, premium dramatic atmosphere, rich deep shadows, crisp details without muddy haze.
Constraints: the image is artwork only. No words, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no UI cards or panels, no buttons, no fake interface screenshot. No extra people.
